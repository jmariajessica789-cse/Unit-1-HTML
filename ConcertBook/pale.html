About the Event

Pale White Dot follows Ram and Lakshmi, a young couple navigating love, ambition, and societal expectations in contemporary urban India.

As they prepare for the next step in their marriage, the mysterious arrival of the "Pale White Dot" brings unspoken fears to the surface. Through music that emerges from ordinary moments, the story captures the emotional complexities of modern relationships.
