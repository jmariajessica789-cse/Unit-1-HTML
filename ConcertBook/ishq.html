About the Event

Ishq Mastana is a soulful concert by Bindhumalini and Vedanth Bharadwaj that celebrates love and devotion through Bhakti and Sufi musical traditions.

Drawing from the poetry of mystic saints like Mirabai, Kabir, Bulleh Shah, and Amir Khusro, the performance explores love in all its forms—longing, surrender, ecstasy, and divine union. It's an intimate, meditative experience that transcends time and boundaries.

"A shared, reflective cultural experience rather than just a concert."
