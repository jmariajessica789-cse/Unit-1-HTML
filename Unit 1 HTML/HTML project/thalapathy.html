About the Event

Thalapathy Sing-Along Night - Vintage hits of Thalapathy
Join us for a nostalgic evening celebrating the timeless charm of Thalapathy's vintage hits. The
Thalapathy Sing-Along Night Vintage Special is happening on Sunday, February 22nd at Arangam,
bringing together fans for a night filled with music, memories, and pure vibe.
This event features an open mic session where fans are free to sing, speak, perform, or express anything
inspired by Thalapathy. Whether you want to relive iconic songs, share your admiration, or simply enjoy
the collective energy, this space is open to everyone.
Come be a part of a warm, vintage-themed evening dedicated to celebrating Thalapathy together,
loud, and from the heart.
