About the Event

Pattu Padava - Live Music Concert for a Cause 🎶
Join us for Pattu Padava, a soulful live music concert dedicated to making a meaningful difference.
This event is organized as a fundraising initiative to provide hearing aids for two children in need,
helping them experience the world of sound with clarity and joy.
Come, be a part of this beautiful cause as talented artists take the stage to fill the evening with melody,
emotion, and purpose.
Your presence and support can help transform lives.
Let's come together, enjoy great music, and create a positive impact. ❤️🎵
